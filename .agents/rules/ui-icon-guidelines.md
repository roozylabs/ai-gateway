# UI Iconography & Aesthetics Guidelines

This rule strictly governs icon selection and visual design across all RoozyLabs Prism web applications (`apps/app`, `apps/web`).

## Directives

1. **Absolute Prohibition of Generic `Sparkles` / Magic AI Icons**:
   - **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN**: Never import or render `Sparkles` (or `✨` magic sparkle icons) anywhere in the application (including buttons, auto-fill triggers, badges, page headers, section titles, card titles, micro-actions, or navigation items).
   - Cliché AI sparkles give the product an amateur, template-generated "AI slop" appearance rather than a high-performance, enterprise-grade AI control plane.

2. **Approved System, Infrastructure & Control-Plane Iconography**:
   - **Auto-Fill & Suggestions**: Use `Zap`, `SlidersHorizontal`, `RotateCcw`, `Cpu`, or `Wrench`.
   - **Activity & Telemetry Logs**: Use `Activity`, `Terminal`, `FileText`, or `History`.
   - **Models & Compute Engine**: Use `Cpu`, `Layers`, `Box`, or `Zap`.
   - **Routing Policies & Rules**: Use `SlidersHorizontal`, `Workflow`, or `GitBranch`.
   - **Governance, Auth & Keys**: Use `ShieldCheck`, `Shield`, `KeyRound`, `Lock`, or `UserCheck`.
   - **Agents & Integrations**: Use `Bot`, `Code2`, or `Plug`.

3. **Visual Alignment & Sizing**:
   - Icons in card headers, buttons, or dropdown labels must maintain uniform sizing (`h-3.5 w-3.5`, `h-4 w-4` or `h-5 w-5`) and consistent padding to prevent baseline misalignments.
