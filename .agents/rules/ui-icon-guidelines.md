# UI Iconography & Aesthetics Guidelines

This rule governs icon selection and visual design across all RoozyLabs Prism web applications (`apps/app`, `apps/web`).

## Directives

1. **Prohibition of Generic `Sparkles` / Magic AI Icons**:
   - **DO NOT** use `Sparkles` (or ✨ magic sparkle icons) in page headers, section titles, card titles, or navigation items.
   - Cliché AI icons give the product an artificial, template-generated appearance rather than a production-grade enterprise control plane.

2. **Use System, Infrastructure & Control-Plane Iconography**:
   - **Activity & Telemetry Logs**: Use `Activity`, `Terminal`, `FileText`, or `History`.
   - **Models & Compute Engine**: Use `Cpu`, `Layers`, `Box`, or `Zap`.
   - **Routing Policies & Rules**: Use `SlidersHorizontal`, `Workflow`, or `GitBranch`.
   - **Governance, Auth & Keys**: Use `ShieldCheck`, `KeyRound`, `Lock`, or `UserCheck`.
   - **Agents & Integrations**: Use `Bot`, `Code2`, or `Plug`.

3. **Visual Alignment**:
   - Icons used in card headers or dropdown labels must maintain equal heights (`h-4 w-4` or `h-5 w-5`) and consistent padding to avoid label baseline misalignments.
